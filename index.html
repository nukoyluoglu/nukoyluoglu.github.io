PhD Candidate · Harvard University

Nazlı Uğur Köylüoğlu

I'm a PhD candidate in Quantum Science and Engineering at Harvard. As a theorist in the Lukin Group, I co‑design algorithms and architectures for quantum hardware to make the most of scarce resources, across quantum simulation, sensing, and fault-tolerant computation. I work closely with experimental teams building neutral-atom quantum computers and solid-state spin sensors.

I have also worked on quantum algorithms and applications at Google Quantum AI and IBM Quantum. Before my PhD, I studied physics, computer science, mathematics, and history at Stanford.

I'm excited about using AI to accelerate quantum computing research. My work is supported in part by Anthropic's AI for Science program.
